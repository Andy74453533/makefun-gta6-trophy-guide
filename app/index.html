Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough, about 200 hours (PowerPyx; Push Square says 250 or more). No online trophies, no DLC, no difficulty settings, no mission replay — but free roam continues after the story, and every trophy can be earned in that one run.

Missable trophies

Looks missable, but is not

  • Every story trophy — the 12 chapter and mission trophies unlock automatically and none can be missed.
  • Stranger quest givers who go quiet — Kenton, for one, disappears for part of the story. Every trophy-related quest giver comes back and stays after the story.
  • Optional missions that vanish — many disappear after a while, but there are far more than the 100 the checklist needs.
  • Timed quests (hourglass icon) — they can fail on a timer, but none is tied to a trophy.
  • Fences that leave for good — none of them has a trophy quest.
  • Magazine collectibles in story buildings — they also spawn elsewhere.
  • The Irredeemable criminal profile — permanent, and it makes the police more aggressive, but it does not lock any trophy.

Source: PowerPyx's trophy guide and roadmap; Push Square lists no known missables. Save advice from PowerPyx: the game has 15 manual save slots plus 1 autosave, and missions cannot be replayed, so keep a save at the start and end of each chapter.

About this guide

What it is. A free, open-source, offline trophy guide and tracker for Grand Theft Auto VI on PlayStation 5. It covers all 55 official trophies — 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze, 28 of them hidden until earned — with the official name, description and grade of each, a checklist saved in your browser, a roadmap to the platinum, and a guide to each trophy in our own words, with its sources named beside it.

How the guides were written. Every guide is written from the official trophy descriptions and the published guides cited beside it — PowerPyx, Push Square, KeenGamer, GTABase and RadioTimes, with the official list itself from Exophase and PSNProfiles. Nothing is copied from those guides: a source is cited, never copied. Where the guide writers have not yet pinned a method down (five trophies at launch), the guide says so plainly instead of inventing a step.

What we do not claim. The game is a day old and nobody on this guide's team has played it through. Anything marked “not yet confirmed” is exactly that. Check the cited source for the latest word, and treat a guide that claims certainty about an unconfirmed trophy with suspicion.

Privacy. Your progress lives only in this browser (localStorage). Export writes a JSON file on your machine; import reads one back. The app makes no network request at all — the source links open only if you click them.
